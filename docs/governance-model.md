# Governance Model

## Governance Principles

The solution follows a governance-first operating model.

Mandatory rules:

- Human accountability remains required
- AI operates as advisory support only
- Operational decisions require analyst validation
- AI scope remains bounded
- Operational actions remain auditable

---

## Operational Boundaries

Allowed:

- Guided troubleshooting
- Incident normalization
- Diagnostic assistance
- Decision support

Not Allowed:

- Autonomous remediation
- Ticket closure
- Privileged actions
- Identity modifications
- Unapproved AI integrations
